Deleting your Unfog account You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app: 1. Open Unfog and tap your profile picture 2. Open the settings menu 3. Choose Delete Account and confirm This is immediate and permanent. It removes your travel history (countries, landmarks and dates), every photo you uploaded, your points and badges, your friends and friend requests, and your profile including your username. Deleting your Unfog account does not affect your Facebook, Google or Apple account. If you cannot access the app, email mtstudio.ios@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.
