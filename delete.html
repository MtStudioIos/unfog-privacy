Unfog: Travel Tracker & Map — Account Deletion

How to remove your account and everything in it.

From inside the app

  1. Open Unfog and tap your profile picture
  2. Open the settings menu
  3. Choose Profile Settings
  4. Scroll down and choose Delete My Account, then confirm
This is immediate and permanent. It removes your travel history (countries, landmarks and dates), every photo you uploaded, your points and badges, your friends and friend requests, and your profile including your username. It cannot be undone, and we cannot restore it afterwards.

What it does not affect

Deleting your Unfog account does not affect your Google, Facebook or Apple account. It only removes the account you have with us.

If you cannot open the app

Email mtstudio.ios@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Guest accounts. A guest account has no email address attached to it, so email us your guest username instead — it looks like Traveler_6641 and is shown on your profile.

Because guest usernames are visible to other users on the leaderboard, knowing one is not proof that it is yours. We will ask you to confirm a few details only the account holder would know — several of the places you have checked in, for example — before we delete anything. This is to stop somebody deleting a stranger's travel history, which cannot be undone.
