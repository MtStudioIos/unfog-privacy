Common questions

  • How do I add a country or landmark? Tap a country or a landmark pin on the map, then choose "Been Here" to check in.
  • How do I add photos to a trip? When you check in at a place, you can attach a photo to that visit. Your photos are private to you.
  • How do I edit or delete an entry? Open your profile statistics to update travel dates or remove a country or landmark.
  • How do I delete my account? Go to your profile settings and choose "Delete My Account." This permanently removes your profile, travel data, and photos.
